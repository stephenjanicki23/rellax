Betting Edge

Where the engine's own chances disagree with the betting odds. The engine never sees the odds — they are only compared afterwards, so its picks stay independent.

Read this as a shortlist to research, not a list of bets. A very large gap more often means the engine is missing something the market knows (an injury, a bad weight cut, a debutant's pre-UFC record) than a free bet.
